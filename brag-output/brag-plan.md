# Margin launch video plan

**Angle:** the product's best moment is silence. You highlight one confusing sentence, the rest of the paper fades, and help appears beside the page.
**Hook:** "Stuck on one sentence?" over a real, dense page of a real paper.
**Highlights:** the veil, the explanation beside the page, the drawn diagram.
**Punchline:** your papers stay on this computer.
**Tone:** polished, calm, restrained, with soft transitions and long holds.
**Identity:** the app's own colors (mist #F1F3F2, ink #1D1D1F, sage #2F6F6B, mint highlight) and Apple system type.
**Source:** the real built app, driven frame by frame, on page 4 of "Attention Is All You Need" (page 1 has author emails, so it's not shown). The explanation and diagram are real output from gemma4:e4b on this computer, replayed faster than real time.

**Tempo:** 80 BPM, one beat = 0.75s, 28 beats = 21s. Every event below is on a beat.

| Time | Beats | Scene | Caption |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.00-3.00 | 0-4 | Paper; cursor drags across the sentence | Stuck on one sentence? |
| 3.00-5.25 | 4-7 | Explain button; click at 4.50; veil and sheet | Highlight it. |
| 5.25-7.50 | 7-10 | Veil settles, status messages | Everything else goes quiet. |
| 7.50-9.75 | 10-13 | Explanation streams | An explanation appears beside the page. |
| 9.75-12.75 | 13-17 | Answer finishes; follow-up options | Ask for simpler, deeper, or an example. |
| 12.75-16.50 | 17-22 | Click Visualize at 12.75; diagram at 14.25 | Or see it drawn. |
| 16.50-18.75 | 22-25 | Push in on "This computer" | Your papers stay on this computer. |
| 18.75-21.00 | 25-28 | Name card | Margin. A calm reader for research papers. Run it locally with ./run.sh |

**Music:** D major, felt piano arpeggios, warm pad, sub bass, a soft pulse once the veil falls. Effects are tuned to the key and share the same reverb.
